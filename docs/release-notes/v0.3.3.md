Fixes for the "Up next" card, the time under the mouse on the seek bar, and the version moved to the top of Settings.

## What's new

- **Up next during the credits.** The *Up next* card now appears once an episode counts as watched (95%, usually the credits) instead of only at the very end. Its countdown pauses while the video is paused. **Cancel** now lets the episode play to the end before going back to the library, and if you seek back before the credits, the card appears again later.
- **Time on the seek bar.** Hover over the seek bar to see the time at that point.
- **Version at the top of Settings.** The app's version now sits next to the *Settings* title instead of at the bottom.

## Fixes

- **Play now** on the *Up next* card starts the next episode straight away. Before, it did nothing except restart the countdown.
- Clicking the control bar below the video no longer plays or pauses. Only clicking the picture does.
