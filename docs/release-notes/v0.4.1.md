Videos opened from Explorer now remember where you stopped, and closing them never shows the library.

## Changes

- **Videos outside your library remember where you left off.** Close a video you opened from Explorer partway through, and the next time you open it Dropreel asks whether to resume from there or start over, just like movies in your library. Dropreel remembers the last 1,000 such videos.
- **Closing goes straight back to where you were.** When Dropreel was opened just to play a video, closing the player (the ✕ button, `Esc`, or the end of the video) now closes Dropreel without showing the library for a moment first. Closing the "Resume from…" question closes it too.
- **Several videos opened together** keep playing one after another even when one of them asks where to resume, and stay in fullscreen while it does.

## Note

This version updates the library database. After running it, older versions of Dropreel can't open your library, so keep using 0.4.1 or newer.
