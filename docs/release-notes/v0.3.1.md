Posters now open their details instead of playing, and the TMDb key is easier to paste.

## What's new

- **Clicking a poster opens its details.** Movies now work like shows: click a poster to see its details, and use the **▶** button in the middle of the poster to start playback. The ⓘ button in the corner is gone. Right-clicking the poster or its ▶ button opens the same menu as before.
- **Pasting the TMDb key.** In *Settings → TMDb*, the key field has a **Paste** button and a **Show/Hide** toggle. Right-click the field for **Paste**, **Copy** and **Clear**. Spaces at either end of a pasted key are removed.
