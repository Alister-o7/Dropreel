A small change to how DropReel saves an episode when the next one starts.

## Changes

- **Play now and the Up next countdown save your place.** Moving on to the next episode now works the same way however you do it: the ⏭ *Next episode* button, **N**, *Play now*, or letting the countdown run out. The episode you're leaving keeps its place so you can resume it, and it's only marked as watched if you were past 95%. Before, *Play now* and the countdown always marked it as watched.
