A quick fix for the *Up next* card.

## Fixes

- **Up next appears in the last minute.** For episodes shorter than ten minutes, it appears in the last tenth instead. Before, it appeared from 95% of the episode. Episodes are still marked watched at 95%.
- **Up next shows in fullscreen.** When the mouse sat still, as it usually does in fullscreen, the card faded out along with the player controls, and only the mouse pointer showed. The card now stays visible while the controls are hidden.
