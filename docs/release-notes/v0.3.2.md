Small player improvements: click the picture to pause, a clearer volume level, and the app's version in Settings.

## What's new

- **Click the picture to pause.** As on Netflix, a click anywhere on the video plays or pauses it, and a play or pause icon briefly appears in the middle of the screen. Double-clicking still switches fullscreen on and off.
- **Volume level while you change it.** The volume percentage now appears next to the slider whenever the volume changes, whether you drag or click the slider or press ↑ / ↓. It fades out a moment later. Before, it only showed as a tooltip, which didn't appear while dragging.
- **Version in Settings.** The bottom of *Settings* shows which version of DropReel you're running.
