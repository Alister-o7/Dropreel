Tag editing, a player that gets out of the way, and smooth motion off for new installs.

## What's new

- **Rename and delete tags.** Right-click a tag in the sidebar, or use the new buttons in *Settings → Tags*. Renaming a tag to a name that already exists merges the two. Deleting a tag removes it from its movies; the movies stay in the library.
- **The player's controls hide while paused too.** Once the mouse rests, the controls and pointer fade away whether the movie is playing or paused. Move the mouse to bring them back.
- **Choose when the controls hide.** *Settings → Player → Hide the controls after* sets the delay, from 1 to 30 seconds (2.5 by default).
- **Smooth motion is off on fresh installs.** *High-quality video scaling* stays on. If you already use DropReel, your settings stay as they are.
