Quality presets for re-encoding, and a player clock that shows the time left.

## What's new

- **Re-encoding quality presets.** *Settings → Re-encoding → Quality* has three choices:
  - **Best quality** (the default on new installs) uses x265 on the CPU at its slow preset, in 10-bit for smoother gradients. Files look sharper at the same size, but a movie can take several hours.
  - **Fast** uses your graphics card when it can, and otherwise x265 at its fast preset.
  - **Custom** lets you choose the encoder and x265 preset yourself, as before.
- **See the size re-encodes aim for.** Next to *Suggest re-encoding above*, Settings now shows the size a re-encode targets: 95% of the limit, so about 4.75 GB for 5 GB. Change the limit and the target moves with it.
- **Time left in the player.** *Settings → Player → Time shown* switches between the time left (the default on new installs) and the time played and length.

If you already use DropReel, nothing changes until you pick something new in Settings. Re-encoding stays on *Fast*, or on *Custom* if you had chosen an encoder or preset, and the player keeps showing the time played and length.
