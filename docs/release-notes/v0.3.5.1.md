A follow-up fix for the moment one episode hands over to the next.

## Fixes

- **"Loading…" now shows every time the next episode starts.** In 0.3.5 it only showed sometimes, and the previous episode's last frame could still stay on screen until the new episode started playing. Now the screen stays black with *Loading…* until the new episode is actually playing.
