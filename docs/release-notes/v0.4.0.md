Dropreel can now be your video player: open videos straight from Explorer, one window for everything, and player settings right in the player.

## New

- **Open videos from Explorer.** A video opened with *Open with → Dropreel* (or by double-clicking, once Dropreel is your default player) just plays. It isn't added to your library, and closing the player (`Esc` or the ✕ button) closes Dropreel when it was opened just for that video. Videos already in your library play from there, from where you stopped.
- **Set Dropreel as your default video player.** The installer now lists Dropreel under **Settings → Apps → Default apps**, so you can choose it for `.mkv`, `.mp4` and the other video types. It still doesn't take over any file type by itself. The **Choose default video player…** button in the player settings takes you there.
- **One window.** Opening a video while Dropreel is running plays it in the window that's already open, instead of starting a second Dropreel.
- **Open several videos at once.** Select a few videos in Explorer and open them together: they play one after another, in name order.
- **Player settings in the player.** The ⚙ button at the top right of the player opens its settings: video scaling, smooth motion, how long before the controls hide, how the time is shown, and what happens to videos opened from Explorer. Changes apply straight away.
- **Add opened videos to the library instead.** Prefer the old behaviour? Choose **Videos opened from Explorer → Add them to the library** in the player settings or in Settings.

## Fixes

- Changing a setting in the player never overwrites a settings file that couldn't be read (for example, one edited by hand), so your watched folders and TMDb key stay safe.
